## What “body” measures

Here body means the fundamental's share of H1+H2+H3 energy, with H2/H1 and
H3/H1 reported separately. It does not identify a missing structural body
resonance. Raw and whole-crop-matched partial envelopes remain available, so
a larger ratio cannot conceal a disappearing note.

Four Guitar-TECHS P3 phrases retain all 14 registered attacks and 10 held
windows. Seven isolated references add 14 held windows. The candidate uses
the shipping baseline's exact crop and fixed masks. Each full crop receives
one constant gain; there is no per-note/window gain, EQ, time warp, denoising
or candidate alignment. The [portable numeric record](tone-body-comparison-2026-10-18.json)
contains all 24 body rows, 42 real-phrase attack windows and 333 performance
windows covering all 111 registered synthetic onsets.

## Initial spectrum versus subsequent loss

A band can decay more slowly because excitation gives more weight to its
slower partials. Initial power, band slope and individual modal decay answer
different questions. Static linear output EQ cannot change an isolated mode's
exponential decay constant.

Trajectories use fixed windows of 80ms or eight periods, whichever is longer,
stepped 20ms from 80ms after annotation. They stop at 600ms and 30ms before
NoteOff, next onset or crop end. Blackman-Harris band power is checked against
the same pre-onset background, estimated H1–H3 leakage and a Hann result.
A frozen common Reference/shipping prefix must clear background and leakage
by 12dB and agree within 6dB between tapers. A slope needs six frames spanning
at least 200ms. These are observability guards, not perceptual thresholds.
No candidate changes the admitted frames; failed or too-short prefixes stay
in the receipts. The selected candidate has zero failures on admitted frames.

Synthetic witnesses recover H1 share 0.8 as 0.79999924 and a known upper-line
slope −12dB/s as −11.999999997. A pure low-partial signal is rejected as false
4–8k sustain. Unmodeled leakage and nonstationary noise remain possible.

## Selected physical changes and scope

The ordinary modal-release source uses a standard second-order Bessel shape.
Unit DC area and the old two-real-pole source's exact DC group delay determine
its normalization; the tail uses actual digital-pole decay within the existing
truncation budget. No amplitude makeup, fitted bass shelf or output EQ is
added. Equal area and mean do not imply equal energy or peak. Compound
Hammer/Pinch/Dead projections retain their established source path.

The upper-string loss correction references the existing fresh-string loss
law, preserving old age and hand increments. A bounded two-pole low shelf
corrects curvature between the same fundamental and 3.6kHz traversal anchors;
strings 0/1 retain their material-loss path. Continuous affine depth is limited
using exact extrema of a cubic in cosine before temporary hand attenuation.
Invalid bilinear coordinates or a non-Schur denominator bypass and clear the
correction's three histories. The resulting magnitude/stability certificate
applies to that fixed filter, not the entire time-varying coupled instrument.

Small picked fret changes retain each actual raw endpoint and its complete
running feedback-filter bank while the existing C2 handoff runs. Queuing a
new destination cannot refit an audible bank. Hammer/Slide cancellation adopts
the heard incoming bank and performed geometry; expression transports physical
period independently of each bank's phase. Ordinary stopped repicks open the
old stop smoothly over the existing Contact clock. No delay-ring duplication
or new fitted transition duration is introduced.

Palm remains deliberately scoped: the selected source retains Bessel Palm
projection and established hand opening at actual pick arrival. Finite Palm
opening suppressed the old unmute packet but failed the unchanged processed
rapid-Palm upper-body rail. Restoring that established arrival behavior retains
a known packet; it is not evidence of improved upper sustain. Large fret hops
also retain the established instantaneous .28 whole-state choke. The rejected
large-hop bank experiment failed to improve both observed transient witnesses.
Neither limitation is hidden behind a claim that all attacks are smaller.

[Smith's wave-variable treatment](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/Ideal_Plucked_String.html)
supports auditing initialization and observation together.
[Paiva et al.](https://research.aalto.fi/en/publications/acoustics-and-modeling-of-pickups/)
separate spatial pickup effects, aperture, mixing, electrical resonance and
magnetic nonlinearity. Pickup-only controls narrow part of the P3 gap without
identifying its selector or justifying a default change.
[Bank and Välimäki](https://home.mit.bme.hu/~bank/publist/spl03.pdf) distinguish
loop magnitude's effect on decay from phase's effect on modal frequency.
